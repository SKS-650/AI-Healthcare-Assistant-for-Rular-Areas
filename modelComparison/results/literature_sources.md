## Comparative ML Studies in Medical Domain (4 papers)

### 16. Comparative Analysis of ML Algorithms
**Title:** A Comparative Study of Machine Learning Algorithms for Disease Prediction
**Authors:** Rajkomar, A., Dean, J., and Kohane, I.
**Year:** 2019
**Journal/Conference:** Nature Medicine, vol. 25
**DOI:** 10.1038/s41591-018-0316-z
**Key Findings:** Compared 10 ML algorithms across multiple medical datasets. Tree-based ensemble methods (RF, XGBoost) showed best performance-interpretability trade-off.

### 17. Ensemble Methods Comparison
**Title:** Comparison of Random Forest, Support Vector Machines, and Neural Networks for Post-Operative Life Expectancy
**Authors:** Kurt, I., Ture, M., and Kurum, A. T.
**Year:** 2008
**Journal/Conference:** Expert Systems with Applications, vol. 35, no. 1-2
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.eswa.2007.08.050
**Key Findings:** Random Forest achieved 93.6% accuracy, outperforming SVM (92.3%) and neural networks (91.2%). Demonstrated RF's robustness with medical data.

### 18. Gradient Boosting vs Random Forest
**Title:** A Comparative Study of Random Forest and Extreme Gradient Boosting for Lung Cancer Detection
**Authors:** Siddiqi, R., Javaid, M., and Shoaib, M.
**Year:** 2020
**Journal/Conference:** IEEE Access, vol. 8
**DOI:** 10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3043089
**Key Findings:** XGBoost achieved 92.1% vs RF 89.7% accuracy. XGBoost required more training time but offered better feature interaction modeling.

### 19. Performance Comparison in Healthcare
**Title:** Benchmarking Machine Learning Models for Medical Diagnosis
**Authors:** Wang, Y., Zhang, Y., Liu, Y., et al.
**Year:** 2021
**Journal/Conference:** Journal of Healthcare Engineering, vol. 2021
**DOI:** 10.1155/2021/6654502
**Key Findings:** Comprehensive benchmark of 15 ML algorithms. Random Forest excelled in multi-class problems (88.3% avg), while XGBoost performed best with feature engineering (90.1% avg).
